Snipe Hunter

Data merge for After Effects. Version out using spreadsheet data to replace images/videos, text, colors, or anything you can imagine. Save config files and use in Snipe Buddy, a companion app that let's anyone with an Excel file create the versions they need with a click of a button.

Matchbook

Easy asset delivery and versioning. A client needs a specific size of their logo, with Matchbook, they can create it quickly—using the brand colors without having to look up hex codes. Access brand fonts and colors, and download master .ai files from one single link

03

Phosphor

An expression editing IDE. A much larger interface for writing expressions that includes IDE coloring and an expression library for you to save and re-use your favorites. Create an easy expression that allows you to edit placeholders more quickly then scrolling through your entire script and highlighting and retyping values.

04

Striker

Batch Mogrt exports so you don't have to wait. No endless modals to approve, just select the ones you want and export. Grab a cup of coffee and come back to your pile of Mogrts. Re-import mogrts into your current AE project, and preview animated thumbnails of mogrts you've exported. Honestly, all the mogrt features that After Effects should ship natively.

05

Spark

A YouTube Player panel for After Effects. Follow along with tutorials, adding notes and bookmarks that you can reference anytime. Save your favorite methods to reference with ease, or put on your favorite content creator as background noise as you get in the flow state.
